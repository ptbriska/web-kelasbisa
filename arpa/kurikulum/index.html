ARPA (Article Research & Publication Assistance)

Abstract

ARPA is designed as an integrated ecosystem for research competency, scientific writing, and academic publication development. The framework combines structured learning, mentoring systems, and practical academic experience to support students, researchers, and academic communities in developing measurable scientific competencies.
